Social media has become a vital part of personal branding and business growth. But managing your Instagram followers can be overwhelming—especially when you’re trying to identify who’s genuinely engaging with your content. Enter our game-changing Chrome extension that makes managing your Instagram followers effortless.

### The Problem: Following Accounts That Don’t Follow Back

Over time you follow hundreds of accounts. Many of them never followed you back, and some may have unfollowed you since. Checking them one by one takes hours.

Our Chrome extension solves this issue by:

- Listing every account you follow that doesn’t follow you back.
- Letting you filter that list by verified, private, or no profile photo, so you can review it quickly.
- Unfollowing the accounts you no longer want, at a safe pace.

### Key Features

- **One-Click Scan**: Click “Show unfollowers” in the side panel and Unfolks checks every account you follow.
- **Filters and Sorting**: Filter by verified, not verified, private, or no profile photo, and sort by when you followed each account.
- **Privacy First**: No password and no Unfolks account. Your lists stay in your browser.

### Why This Matters

A clean and engaged follower list is essential for:

- **Building Trust**: Show your audience you value genuine connections.
- **Boosting Performance**: Increase visibility and engagement by focusing on real followers.
- **Growing Strategically**: Attract a more targeted audience for your brand or content.

### Easy Steps to Get Started

1. **Install the Extension**: Download it from the <a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/idgjpjkoddplmbdepekendpdbaibcgpc" target="_blank">Chrome Web Store</a>.
2. **Open Instagram**: Sign in to Instagram in Chrome as usual. Unfolks uses that session and never asks for your password.
3. **Scan & Act**: Click the Unfolks icon, then “Show unfollowers”. Review the list and unfollow the accounts you no longer want.
