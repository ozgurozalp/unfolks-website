### Why You Need This Chrome Extension

In today’s social media-driven world, having a clean and active followers list is crucial for growing your Instagram presence. Here’s why our Chrome extension is a game-changer:

- **Identify Non-Followers**: Instantly see who doesn’t follow you back, so you can unfollow inactive or uninterested accounts.
- **Save Time**: No more manual checks! The extension automates the process, giving you more time to focus on creating content.
- **Improve Engagement**: By keeping only genuine followers, you’ll boost your engagement rates and reach a more targeted audience.

### How It Works

Our Chrome extension is designed to be simple and user-friendly. Follow these steps to get started:

1. **Download the Extension**: Head to the <a href="https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/idgjpjkoddplmbdepekendpdbaibcgpc" target="_blank">Chrome Web Store</a> and install the extension for free.
2. **Open Instagram**: Sign in to Instagram in your browser as usual. The extension uses that session and never asks for your password.
3. **Scan Your Account**: Click the Unfolks icon to open the side panel, then click “Show unfollowers” to get a list of users who don’t follow you back.
4. **Take Action**: Unfollow accounts one at a time, or select several and let Unfolks unfollow them at a safe pace.

### Features That Stand Out

- **Fresh Results**: Refresh the list whenever you like, and Unfolks tells you how many accounts may have unfollowed you since the last scan.
- **Private by Design**: Your lists stay on your computer. Unfolks has no server that receives your Instagram data.
- **Filters and Sorting**: Filter non-followers by verified, not verified, private, or no profile photo, sort them by when you followed them, and hide the accounts you want to keep.
